How this tool works

1. Time yourself while reading.
2. Upload a sample page (or each page) or just enter how many pages you read.
3. The app estimates how many words you read and calculates your words per minute.

Levels (assuming normal difficulty text):

  • Beginner: under 150 wpm
  • Intermediate: 150–250 wpm
  • Advanced: 250–350 wpm
  • Expert: 350–450 wpm
  • Master: 450+ wpm
