Reading Speed Analyzer

Uses your Gemini API key to count words in uploaded page images. Images are compressed for speed; multiple pages are analyzed in a single API call.

Quick guide:

  • 1. Time (mandatory): enter how long you read (minutes and/or seconds).
  • 2. Pages: either
    • Upload 1 page and fill “Total pages read” if it’s a sample, or
    • Upload several pages (tool counts each), or
    • No images: enter “Manual page count” (uses 300 words/page).
  • 3. Click “Calculate Reading Speed” to see total words, WPM, and your reading level.

Reading Level –

How reading levels are defined (assuming good comprehension):

  • Beginner (0–149 WPM) – building basic reading fluency.
  • Intermediate (150–249 WPM) – around typical adult reading speed.
  • Advanced (250–349 WPM) – faster than average, efficient reader.
  • Expert (350–499 WPM) – significantly above average, strong focus.
  • Master (500+ WPM) – very rare; trained speed-reader territory.
