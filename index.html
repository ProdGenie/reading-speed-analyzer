How this tool works

Inputs

  • Time: Minutes and seconds you spent reading.
  • Images: Upload either one sample page (plus total pages), or every page you read.
  • Manual pages: If you don't use images, we estimate with 300 words per page.

Output

  • Total words read in your session.
  • Words per minute (WPM).
  • A reading speed category (Beginner → Master).

Each calculation is fresh. Images are sent to Gemini through a Netlify function using your API key on the server; nothing is stored between runs.
